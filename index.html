How ShoSafe Works

Verify the visitor. Protect the agent. Report to the seller.

No clipboard. No guessing who walked through. Every open house runs the same way, every time, and everyone walks out with a record of who was there.

1

Visitors check in by QR

One code at the door. Every visitor registers their name, phone, and whether they're working with an agent. Same process for everyone, which keeps it compliant and keeps the agent out of the bouncer role. "It's not my policy, it's ShoSafe."

2

The agent sees it live

Confirmed visitors appear on the agent's phone in real time. A documented record of exactly who entered the home, building the foundation for the safety features that protect the agent hosting alone.

3

The seller gets a report

When the open house ends, the homeowner automatically receives a branded ShoSafe report: date, address, every visitor, incident status. No other agent in your market hands a seller that.

The differentiator

The seller report wins the listing.

Safety protects the agent. The seller report sells the agent. Walk into a listing presentation and show the homeowner the exact accountability they get when you host their open house. It's a safety tool and a closing tool in one, and nobody else offers it.
